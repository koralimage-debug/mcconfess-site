About

From Nigeria to Atlanta

MC Confess is the stage name of Olushola John Adebowale, a Nigerian-born, Atlanta-based musician, MC and multi-instrumentalist. His sound blends Afrobeat, hip-hop and Yoruba roots into songs about life, forgiveness and hustle.
